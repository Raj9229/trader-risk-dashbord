## Overview

Trader Risk Dashboard is a responsive React-based dashboard built for the Tradescape Full Stack Developer Assignment. It presents key trading metrics such as balance, PnL, win rate, drawdown, and daily risk exposure in a clean and professional interface.

The application uses mock trade data to calculate portfolio performance and risk levels from user-driven inputs. It is designed to feel like a practical product experience while remaining simple, modular, and easy to understand for interview and assignment review.

## How It Works

- Trade Data: The dashboard uses a mock array of trades that includes asset, side, size, PnL, and status.
- Statistics Calculation: Key metrics such as total PnL, win rate, and trade counts are calculated from the trade dataset.
- Risk Calculation: Drawdown and daily loss values are derived from account limits and trade performance to determine the current risk state.
- Equity Curve: The equity curve is generated by applying each trade PnL to the starting balance over time.
- Performance by Asset: PnL is grouped by asset so traders can quickly identify which instruments are contributing most to performance.

## Product Decision

Performance by Asset was added to make the dashboard more practical for traders. Instead of only viewing overall portfolio performance, users can instantly see which assets are driving gains or losses.

This improves decision-making by helping traders quickly identify whether a position, market segment, or strategy is performing well and where attention may be needed.

## Assignment Questions

1. What is drawdown in trading?

Drawdown is the decline in account value from a previous high point. It measures how much capital has been lost during a period of underperformance.

2. Why would a trader care about remaining drawdown instead of only current PnL?

A trader should care about remaining drawdown because it shows how much risk capacity is left before hitting a predefined risk limit. Current PnL alone does not reveal whether the account is approaching an unacceptable loss threshold.

3. If you had one more day, what improvements would you make?

I would add live data integration, stronger chart interactions, and more detailed analytics such as risk by asset, trade journaling, and exportable reports.
